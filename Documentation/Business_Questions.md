# Business Questions – Retail Sales Analytics

This project answers the following business questions using SQL.

## Sales Analysis

1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. What is the total number of orders?
3. What is the average order value?

## Product Analysis

4. Which products generate the highest revenue?
5. Which product categories perform best?
6. Which products have the lowest sales?

## Customer Analysis

7. Who are the top customers by spending?
8. Which customers have placed the most orders?

## Order Analysis

9. How many orders are Completed, Pending, and Cancelled?
10. What percentage of orders are cancelled?

## Business Goal

Use SQL queries to identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing behavior, and provide insights that can support better business decisions.
